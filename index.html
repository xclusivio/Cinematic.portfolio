04 / The artist

Curiosity is
the craft.

Alexander Vale is a screen and stage actor drawn to layered characters and stories that challenge the way we see one another. His work moves between intimate independent cinema, character-led television, and live performance.

Grounded in close observation and a collaborative spirit, he approaches each role as its own world—built with patience, precision, and an appetite for the unexpected.
